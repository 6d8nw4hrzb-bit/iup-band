Kennywood Policies
Bags
Kennywood allows one bag or backpack no larger than 15″ × 15″ × 15″, or a soft cooler no larger than 12″ × 12″ × 12″, per guest.
Food
Outside food and drink are allowed, but everything must fit inside that one bag or cooler.
